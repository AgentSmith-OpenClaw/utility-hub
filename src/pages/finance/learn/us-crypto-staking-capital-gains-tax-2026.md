# Stop Overpaying: Calculating US Capital Gains on Crypto Staking in 2026


Staking crypto is a fantastic way to generate yield, but the IRS treats it with a heavy hand. In 2026, the guidance is finally solidifying, and I see too many investors miscalculating their cost basis and overpaying their tax bills by thousands of dollars.

## Income Tax vs. Capital Gains

When you receive staking rewards, that is ordinary income based on the fair market value of the token the exact moment you receive it. I cannot stress this enough: *receipt is the taxable event for income*.

When you later sell that staked reward, that triggers capital gains or losses. Your cost basis is the amount you reported as ordinary income.

## The Cost Basis Nightmare

If you are staking daily (like on Solana or Ethereum validators), you have 365 taxable income events per year. 

*   **FIFO vs. LIFO:** You must stick to a consistent accounting method. FIFO (First In, First Out) is standard, but Specific ID (HIFO - Highest In, First Out) can drastically reduce your capital gains hit if your platform supports the granular reporting.
*   **Automated Tracking:** Don't do this in a spreadsheet. Use specialized tax software that connects directly via read-only APIs to your wallets.
