# Canada’s 2026 Payroll Changes: Why Your Paycheque is Smaller

If you've noticed your take-home pay feels a bit light lately, you aren't imagining it. 2026 brought a series of adjustments to Canada's tax system that are hitting workers' pockets immediately.

## The CPP and EI Hike

The ceilings for Canada Pension Plan (CPP) and Employment Insurance (EI) have gone up again. While this is supposed to mean better benefits later, the immediate result is more money taken out of every paycheque. For middle and high-income earners, this is a noticeable dent in monthly cash flow.

## The Inflation Indexing Gap

The federal government adjusted tax brackets for inflation, but there's a catch: the "official" inflation rate used for these tweaks often lags behind the actual cost of living increases we’re seeing in cities like Toronto, Vancouver, and Calgary. You might be making more money, but after taxes and rent, you have less to spend.
