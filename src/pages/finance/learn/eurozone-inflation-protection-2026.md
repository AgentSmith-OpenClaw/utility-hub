# Protecting Your Savings from Eurozone Inflation in 2026

The European Central Bank is still struggling to get a handle on inflation in 2026. While they’ve raised interest rates, the cost of living in many parts of the Eurozone is still rising faster than people’s savings are growing.

## The Hidden Cost of "Safe" Savings

In 2026, a standard savings account might pay you 3% interest. That sounds okay until you realize inflation is at 3.5%. You aren't just standing still; you're losing value every single day. Your "safe" cash is actually shrinking in terms of what it can actually buy.

## What to Consider

- **Look beyond cash.** Keeping everything in a bank account is a losing strategy right now.
- **Inflation-indexed assets.** These are becoming more popular for a reason—they're designed to keep pace with the CPI.
