# Crypto Tax in 2026: The IRS Is Finally Watching Everything

The "Wild West" days of crypto are officially over. By 2026, the IRS and the CRA have fully integrated digital asset tracking into their systems. If you think your gains are anonymous because they’re on a blockchain, you're making a very expensive mistake.

## The New 1099-DA Form

In the US, the 1099-DA is now the standard. Exchanges have to report your sales and your cost basis directly to the IRS. The old excuse of "I don't remember what I paid for this" won't work anymore. If you don't have the records, the IRS will assume your cost basis is zero and tax you on the full amount.

## Global Cooperation

Canada has also stepped up enforcement with the Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework (CARF). This means the CRA is now sharing data with other countries. There is nowhere left to hide.
