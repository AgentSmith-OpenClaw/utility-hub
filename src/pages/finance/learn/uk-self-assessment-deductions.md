# The UK Self-Assessment Tax Return: Hidden Deductions

HMRC collects millions in overpaid taxes simply because freelancers and directors fail to claim their legitimate expenses. The 2026 tax year requires tight record-keeping.

## Working From Home

The flat rate is simple (£6 a week), but actual expenses often yield a larger deduction. Calculate the proportion of your home used for business (by rooms or floor area) and apply that percentage to rent, mortgage interest, council tax, electricity, and heating. 

## Equipment and Tech

If you buy a laptop for your freelance work, claim it as a capital allowance. Under the Annual Investment Allowance (AIA), you deduct the full cost from your profits before tax. This applies to desks, chairs, and specialized software. Personal use must be deducted. If you use the laptop 20% for personal browsing, only claim 80% of the cost.

## Professional Subscriptions and Training

You can deduct subscriptions to approved professional bodies. HMRC maintains a specific list of approved organizations (List 3). Training courses are deductible only if they update existing skills. Learning a completely new skill to expand your business is considered capital expenditure and is not deductible.
