# Canada Condo Crash: OSFI Warns on 'Blanket Appraisals' as Market Shifts

**March 12, 2026**

The Canadian real estate market, long considered stable, is facing a turning point. As condo prices in major hubs like Toronto and Vancouver experience a significant pullback, the Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions (OSFI) has issued a critical warning to lenders regarding appraisal practices.

## The Problem with 'Blanket Appraisals'
During the boom, many lenders used blanket appraisals for entire condo developments. As prices crash, these valuations are often 15-20% higher than actual market value, creating "negative equity" risks for new buyers and systemic risk for banks.

### What This Means for Buyers and Investors
If you bought a pre-construction condo in 2023-2024 closing in 2026, you might find that your bank's new appraisal comes in lower than your purchase price. This creates a "funding gap" that you must cover in cash.
