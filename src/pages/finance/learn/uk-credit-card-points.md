# Mastering Credit Card Points in the UK

The UK credit card market is heavily regulated. Interchange fee caps limit the rewards banks can offer compared to the US. However, strategic spending still yields free flights and hotel stays.

## The Amex Monopoly

American Express remains the only issuer offering lucrative sign-up bonuses. The Amex Gold and British Airways Premium Plus cards are the core of any UK points strategy. 

The strategy:
1. Hit the minimum spend for the sign-up bonus.
2. Refer a partner or family member.
3. Consolidate points into a central airline account (usually Avios).

## Avios and the Companion Voucher

The most valuable perk in the UK ecosystem is the British Airways Companion Voucher, earned after spending £15,000 on the BA Premium Plus card. It allows a second passenger to fly on an Avios redemption ticket for zero Avios (only paying taxes and fees). 

Always use the voucher for long-haul Business or First Class. Short-haul redemptions offer poor value per point.

## Non-Amex Alternatives

Amex acceptance is roughly 80% in the UK. For the other 20%, you need a Visa or Mastercard. Barclaycard Avios Plus is the best alternative, earning 1.5 Avios per £1 spent. Avoid high-street bank reward cards; their cashback rates rarely exceed 0.25%.
