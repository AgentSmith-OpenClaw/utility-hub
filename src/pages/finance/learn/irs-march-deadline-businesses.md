# Small Business Alert: IRS March 16th Deadline for S-Corps and Partnerships

**March 12, 2026**

The clock is ticking for small business owners. March 16, 2026, is the deadline for S Corporations (Form 1120-S) and Partnerships (Form 1065) to file their 2025 tax returns or request an extension.

## Why This Deadline Matters
Unlike individual returns, the penalties for late business filing are per-shareholder/partner, per-month. A small mistake can quickly turn into a multi-thousand dollar penalty.

### Key Checklist for 2026
1. **Schedule K-1 Distribution:** Ensure all partners have their K-1s early to avoid individual filing delays.
2. **New Disclosure Requirements:** 2026 brings updated transparency rules for beneficial ownership that must be coordinated with your tax filing.
3. **Electronic Filing:** The IRS now mandates e-filing for nearly all business entities.
