# Debt Snowball vs. Debt Avalanche: Mathematical Comparison and Tools

If you are trying to dig your way out of debt, you have probably encountered the two heavyweights of debt repayment strategies: the Debt Snowball and the Debt Avalanche. Both methods work, but they approach the problem from entirely different angles—psychology vs. mathematics.

Choosing the right method can mean the difference between staying motivated and giving up. Here is a breakdown of how they work, so you can decide which fits your personality.

## The Debt Snowball: The Psychology Play

Popularized by financial personalities, the Debt Snowball focuses on quick wins to build momentum.

**How it works:**
1. List all your debts from smallest balance to largest balance, regardless of interest rate.
2. Pay the minimum on everything.
3. Throw every extra dollar at the smallest debt until it is gone.
4. Take the money you were paying on the first debt and roll it into the next smallest debt.

**Why it works:** Humans are emotional creatures. Seeing a debt disappear entirely gives you a dopamine hit. It provides tangible proof that you are making progress, which keeps you motivated when times get tough. 

## The Debt Avalanche: The Mathematics Play

The Debt Avalanche is for the analytical thinker. It is designed to save you the maximum amount of money on interest.

**How it works:**
1. List all your debts from the highest interest rate to the lowest interest rate.
2. Pay the minimum on everything.
3. Throw every extra dollar at the debt with the highest interest rate.
4. Once that is gone, roll the payments into the debt with the next highest rate.

**Why it works:** By attacking the most expensive debt first, you reduce the total amount of interest that accrues. Mathematically, this guarantees you will pay less money overall and get out of debt slightly faster.

## Which Should You Choose?

Let's look at the numbers. If you have a $500 medical bill at 0% interest and a $5,000 credit card balance at 24% interest, the Avalanche method says attack the credit card. The Snowball method says knock out the medical bill first to feel good about it.

If you are strictly logical and have the discipline to stick to a plan without needing quick wins, choose the **Avalanche**. 
If you have tried to pay off debt before and lost motivation, choose the **Snowball**.
