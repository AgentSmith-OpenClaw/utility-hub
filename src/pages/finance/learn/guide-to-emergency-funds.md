# The Ultimate Guide to Building an Emergency Fund in 2026
Building an emergency fund is crucial for financial stability. This guide covers how much you need, where to store it, and strategies to build it fast.

## Why You Need an Emergency Fund
Unexpected expenses happen. From car repairs to medical bills, having a buffer keeps you out of high-interest debt.

## How Much is Enough?
Aim for 3-6 months of essential living expenses. If your job is volatile, lean towards 6-12 months.
