# The 2026 Auto Loan Trap: Don't Get Fooled by "Low" Monthly Payments

The era of affordable car financing is over. In 2026, the average monthly payment for a new car has topped $750. It’s a combination of high sticker prices and the highest interest rates we’ve seen in two decades.

## The Danger of 84-Month Loans

To make these expensive cars look "affordable," dealers are pushing 84 or 96-month loans. It keeps the monthly payment down, but it’s a trap. By the time you finish paying off the car, you'll have paid thousands—sometimes tens of thousands—in interest alone. Even worse, you'll likely be "underwater" (owing more than the car is worth) for almost the entire time you own it.

## How to Buy Smart

- **Check your credit score first.** A few points can be the difference between a manageable rate and a predatory one.
- **Aim for a 20% down payment.** It’s a big ask, but it's the only way to avoid the worst of the interest trap.
