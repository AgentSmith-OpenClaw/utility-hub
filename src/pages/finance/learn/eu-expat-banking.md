# The Expat's Guide to Banking in the EU: IBANs, Fees, and Transfers

Moving to the European Union requires setting up a reliable banking stack. Traditional banks are slow and charge high fees. Neobanks offer better solutions for expats.

## The IBAN Discrimination Problem

By law (SEPA regulation), any EU IBAN must be accepted across all member states for salary payments and direct debits. In practice, French utility companies often reject Lithuanian IBANs (Revolut) or German IBANs (N26). This illegal practice is called IBAN discrimination.

Keep a local bank account for legacy utilities and use a pan-European neobank for daily spending and currency conversion.

## Choosing the Right Bank

*   **N26:** German IBAN, excellent app, free ATM withdrawals.
*   **Revolut:** Best for multi-currency holding and transferring money back home.
*   **Wise:** Essential for receiving non-EUR income and converting it at the mid-market rate.

## Transferring Large Sums

Never use your home bank to wire money to your new EU account. The hidden exchange rate markups average 3-5%. Use Wise or Interactive Brokers for the actual currency conversion, then transfer the EUR locally.
