# Energy Shocks and Interest Rates: What the Iran War Means for UK Mortgages

**March 12, 2026**

For months, UK homeowners have been watching the Bank of England (BoE) with bated breath, hoping for the long-promised rate cuts. However, the recent eruption of conflict in Iran has sent shockwaves through global energy markets, forcing a dramatic reassessment of the 2026 economic outlook.

## The Energy-Inflation Nexus
The UK remains highly sensitive to global gas prices. The "Iran Shock" of early 2026 has pushed Brent crude back above $100, fueling fears of a secondary inflation spike. For the BoE, this means the "dovish hold" of February is morphing into a "hawkish pause."

### The Impact on Mortgage Renewals
Millions of UK households are set to renew their fixed-rate mortgages in 2026. The expected drop to sub-4% rates is now looking increasingly unlikely. Swap rates—which price fixed-rate mortgages—have already begun to climb in response to the volatility.

## Strategic Planning for Homeowners
Wait or lock in? It's the multi-billion pound question. If energy prices remain elevated, the "Higher for Longer" mantra will return strongly.
