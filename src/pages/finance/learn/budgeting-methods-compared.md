# 50/30/20 vs. Zero-Based Budgeting: Which is Right for You?
Choosing the right budgeting method can make or break your financial goals.

## The 50/30/20 Rule
Simple and flexible: 50% needs, 30% wants, 20% savings/debt payoff.

## Zero-Based Budgeting
Every dollar gets a job. Best for meticulous trackers.
