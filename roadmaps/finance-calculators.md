# Finance Calculators — Roadmap

Next calculators to build, ranked by **AdSense RPM × search demand × build effort**. Finance keywords have some of the highest CPCs on the web — mortgage, insurance, refinance, annuity, and tax queries regularly clear $15–$50+ CPC in the US/UK/AU.

## Tier 1 — High RPM, large global volume (build first)

### US-focused (highest finance CPCs in the world) - Done all implemented
- **Mortgage Refinance Break-Even Calculator** — Refi savings vs closing costs; "should I refinance" intent. *(extends existing refinance variant into a hero page)*
- **HELOC Calculator** — Home-equity line of credit payments, draw vs repayment phase. Very high CPC.
- **Annuity Calculator** — Immediate / deferred annuity payout. Underserved, $20+ CPC keyword set.
- **RMD Calculator** — Required Minimum Distribution from IRA/401(k) at age 73+. Steady seasonal traffic.
- **HSA Calculator** — Health Savings Account triple-tax-advantage projection. High CPC (health + finance).
- **Roth Conversion Calculator** — Tax cost of converting Traditional → Roth IRA. Premium retirement intent.
- **529 College Savings Calculator** — Tax-advantaged college fund growth, multi-state plan comparison.
- **Reverse Mortgage Calculator** — Seniors, very high CPC, low competition for clean tools.
- **Capital Gains Tax Calculator (US)** — Short vs long-term gains, federal + state, NIIT.

### UK / Europe
- **Stamp Duty Calculator (UK)** — SDLT bands for England/NI, LBTT Scotland, LTT Wales. Massive evergreen search.
- **PAYE / UK Take-Home Calculator** — Income tax + NI + student loan + pension. Extremely high volume.
- **ISA Calculator** — Stocks & Shares ISA growth, annual allowance tracking.
- **UK Mortgage Affordability** — Loan-to-income multiples, stress-test rates.
- **VAT Calculator (UK / EU)** — Standalone, per-country preset (already partially covered by Sales Tax tool — promote to dedicated UK page).

### Australia
- **Stamp Duty Calculator (AU)** — Per-state with first-home-buyer concessions. Highest-CPC AU finance keyword set.
- **Superannuation Calculator** — Compulsory + voluntary contributions, retirement projection.
- **HECS-HELP Repayment Calculator** — Income-based student loan repayment.
- **Australian Tax / Take-Home Calculator** — ATO 2025–26 brackets, Medicare levy, HELP, LITO.

### Global / multi-market
- **Currency Converter with Historical Rates** — Evergreen, ad-friendly. Plug-in to existing inflation tool.
- **Crypto Profit / DCA Calculator** — Cost-basis, profit/loss, tax-lot. Strong volume, decent CPC.
- **Loan Comparison Calculator** — Compare 2–3 loan offers side-by-side (rate, term, fees, APR).
- **Salary-by-City Cost of Living** — "What salary do I need in NYC vs Austin?" Top-of-funnel intent.

## Tier 2 — Specialist, lower volume but very high RPM
- **Life Insurance Needs Calculator** — DIME / income-replacement method. Insurance = top-5 CPC vertical.
- **Disability Insurance Needs Calculator** — Long-tail, premium advertiser pool.
- **Estate / Inheritance Tax Calculator** — US estate tax, UK IHT, AU absence — niche but lucrative.
- **Self-Employed / 1099 Tax Calculator (US)** — Quarterly estimated tax, SE tax, deductions.
- **Freelancer Tax Calculator (UK)** — Self-assessment, IR35.
- **Bond Yield Calculator** — YTM, current yield, after-tax yield.
- **ETF Fee Drag Calculator** — Expense-ratio impact over 30 years. Educational hook for investing keywords.
- **Pension Lump-Sum vs Annuity** — Decision tool, very high RPM in US retirement traffic.

## Tier 3 — Volume plays, supports internal linking
- **Net Salary Calculators per country** — Variants for Germany, France, Canada, Singapore, UAE.
- **Property Tax Calculator (US, per state)** — Programmatic SEO, one engine, 50 landing pages.
- **Closing Cost Calculator (US)** — Pairs with mortgage hub.
- **Down Payment Savings Calculator** — Goal-based saving, monthly contribution required.
- **Debt Consolidation Calculator** — Multi-loan → single loan comparison.

## Build sequencing (90-day plan)

1. **Month 1 — US retirement & refi cluster:** Refinance break-even, HELOC, Annuity, RMD, HSA. (Closes biggest CPC gap in our current US lineup.)
2. **Month 2 — UK + AU hubs:** Stamp Duty UK, PAYE, Stamp Duty AU, Superannuation. (Opens two markets where we currently rank for almost nothing.)
3. **Month 3 — Long-tail amplifiers:** 529, Roth Conversion, Capital Gains, Life Insurance Needs, Currency Converter, Crypto DCA. (Feeds Tier 1 with internal links; programmatic state pages start here.)

## SEO leverage notes
- Reuse the existing `finance-variants` pattern — one engine, many slugs (per state, per country) keeps build cost flat while indexable surface compounds.
- Every new calculator needs: dedicated FAQ block, Calculator schema, intent-matched H1, and at least 2 incoming links from related calculators.
- Prioritize keywords where Google's SERP shows third-party calculators (NerdWallet, Bankrate, MoneySavingExpert) — that's a signal advertisers are bidding and ad units perform.
